Pocket Synth Emulator: privacy policy

Pocket Synth Emulator (FM-1 Pocket), Android app io.github.orianbsilva.fm1pocket, by Orian Beltrame. Last updated 16 November 2026. Português

In short

The app collects no personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it sends nothing about you anywhere.

What it connects to, and why

  • thefirmwarehub.com: the list of firmwares (catalog.json), at most once a day, with a User-Agent that names the app.
  • github.com (and GitHub's download servers, raw.githubusercontent.com, and as a last resort api.github.com): the firmware packages you choose to download, from their makers' releases; the makers' pictures; the releases of repositories you add yourself; M-VAVE's original firmware (V15) from M-VAVE's repository.
  • A maker's own web page, only when you open it (a browser version or an editor): that page's own privacy policy applies.

Like any web request, these servers see your IP address and the app's User-Agent. The app sends them nothing else.

What stays on your phone

The firmwares you downloaded, each firmware's saves in the emulator (its presets and settings), the files you open, and the app's settings (for example your controller mappings) are kept in the app's own storage on your phone. They never leave it, and uninstalling the app removes them. A file you open is read only through Android's file picker, and only the file you pick.

USB and MIDI

The app talks only to the devices you connect (an FM-1, a MIDI keyboard or controller), on the phone, to play and to install firmware. Nothing from them is sent anywhere.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Contact

Questions or requests: github.com/orianbsilva/fm1-pocket/issues.

Política de privacidade

O app não coleta dados pessoais: não tem conta, anúncios, analytics nem rastreamento, e não envia nada sobre você.

Ele se conecta a thefirmwarehub.com (a lista de firmwares, no máximo uma vez por dia), ao github.com e aos servidores de download do GitHub (os pacotes que você escolhe baixar, as imagens dos autores, os repositórios que você adiciona, o firmware original da M-VAVE) e à página de um autor só quando você a abre (vale a política daquela página). Esses servidores veem o seu IP e o User-Agent do app, como em qualquer acesso à web; o app não envia mais nada.

Os firmwares baixados, o que cada firmware salva no emulador, os arquivos que você abre e as configurações ficam no armazenamento do próprio app, no celular, e saem dele com a desinstalação. O USB e o MIDI falam só com os aparelhos que você conecta, sem enviar nada para fora.

Contato: github.com/orianbsilva/fm1-pocket/issues.
