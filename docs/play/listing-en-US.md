Pocket Synth Emulator

## Short description (80)
Play M-VAVE FM-1 community firmwares on your phone, with a MIDI keyboard.

## Full description (4000)
Pocket Synth Emulator is an unofficial emulator of the M-VAVE FM-1 pocket synthesizer. It runs the FM-1's community firmwares — synths, drum machines, grooveboxes, generative instruments — on your phone, exactly as they run on the device: their own update packages, unchanged, on an emulated FM-1.

PLAY
• Sideways and full screen: the FM-1's screen, its 8 knobs (drag up or down), its 12 buttons with their lights, and its 27 keys for several fingers — or hide the keys and play from a keyboard.
• Connect a MIDI keyboard (USB through an OTG hub, or Bluetooth) and it plays the firmware, as a cable into the FM-1's MIDI IN would.
• MIDI learn: a controller's knob turns any of the panel's knobs.
• Low-latency sound at the firmware's own 44.1 kHz.
• Offline: once a firmware is on the phone, it plays without the internet. Each firmware version keeps its own presets and settings.

FIRMWARES
• The Firmware Hub's list: every firmware with its maker, text, licence and source. Each package is downloaded from its maker's own release and kept only if it matches the checksum the hub verified.
• Add a GitHub repository for a firmware that is not on the hub yet; new releases arrive by themselves.
• Open a .fwsc file you already have.
• Try the makers' own browser versions, with your MIDI keyboard.

A REAL FM-1 TOO
With a USB-OTG cable, install a firmware on your FM-1, finish an interrupted update, go back to M-VAVE's original firmware, or play it from a MIDI keyboard through the phone.
