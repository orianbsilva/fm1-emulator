Pocket Synth Emulator

An unofficial emulator, for Android, of the M-VAVE FM-1 pocket synthesizer: its community firmwares, their packages unchanged, played on the phone with your fingers, a MIDI keyboard or a controller. The emulation is fm1-emu by Joakim Eriksson (MIT); the firmware list is The Firmware Hub's.
